# Ammonia–Hydrogen Combustion Modeling (2026)

Computational research conducted at Princeton's Computational
Turbulent Reacting Flow Laboratory (CTRFL).
[Combustion Research Report.pdf](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/32447407/Combustion.Research.Report.pdf)

## Overview
The concern of using ammonia-hydrogen fuels for combustion is a popular idea discussed at the Combustion Conference in Kyoto in 2026.
However, what ratio of ammonia? Under what temperature/pressure conditions do smimulations hold? What equivlance ratios are the most efficient?
The summer of 2026 I conducted combustion simulations using the PDRS framework developed by the CTRFL team to test 3 different established models and
the various conditiosn they hold in and published a report of my findings.
In collaboration with my mentor Professor Michael. E. Mueller and Dr. Syndey Rzepka.

## Research Goals
- Determine the "greenest" ratio that produced the least NOx and N2O
- Determine how these findings hold across other models and conditions
- Optimize the running of simulations by contributing to and debugging the existing codebase
